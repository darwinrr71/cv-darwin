**Distance meter with ultrasonic sensor**  
This project demonstrates the use of the ultrasonic sensor **"HC-SR05"** for **distance measurement**. The results are **validated** and presented via an interface consisting of **seven-segment displays** and a **VGA screen**.

The sensor sends out **ultrasonic signals** to detect objects in front of the sensor within a range of **2 to 450 cm**. When the signals bounce off an object and return to the sensor, the distance is calculated. The measurement results are shown on the development board’s **seven-segment displays**, as well as on a **VGA screen** that shifts color between **red, green, blue, and white** depending on the measured distance.

**Accelerometer — Reading + VGA display (DE10-Lite)**  
A system that records data from the board’s **accelerometer** and visualizes the measured values **graphically** on a **VGA screen**.

The program runs on the **DE10-Lite** board, a robust hardware design platform built around the **Altera MAX 10 FPGA** with a **Nios II CPU** that is adapted for engineering work, a **VGA component**, **PIO units** for push buttons and LEDs, as well as an **SPI component** for reading the accelerometer.
